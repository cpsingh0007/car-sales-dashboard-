📌 Project Overview

The Car Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze and visualize car sales performance.
This dashboard helps stakeholders monitor sales trends, pricing insights, regional performance, and company-wise sales through dynamic visualizations.

The project provides both high-level business insights (Overview) and transaction-level details (Details page) for better decision-making.

📊 Dashboard Features

1️⃣ Sales Overview

The Overview dashboard presents a summary of key performance indicators and sales insights:

Visualizations Included

📈 YTD Sales Weekly Trend – Shows how sales changed over time.

🍩 Sales by Body Style – Distribution of sales by car body type.

🍩 Sales by Color – Popular car color sales analysis.

🗺 Cars Sold by Dealer Region – Geographic visualization of dealer performance.
